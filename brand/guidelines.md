# Rough Draft brand guidelines

> Wellness for people who are tired of being sold wellness.

## 1. Essence

Rough Draft is anti-sterile and anti-"self-care influencer". It feels like a zine made by a friend who actually gets it: loud, honest, a little feral, and deeply caring underneath.

The name is the promise. A draft is never finished, belongs to whoever wrote it, and is allowed to be messy. That's true of every person using the app.

## 2. Beliefs, and what each one does to the design

| Belief | So in the product |
|---|---|
| **Wellness is messy, not aspirational.** Nobody is "healed", everybody is in progress. | No streaks, scores, levels, badges for consistency, or "healed" states. Missed days are dashed outlines, never red. |
| **Comfort is not the goal. Shame isn't either.** | Prompts can push ("write the text you won't send"). The UI never punishes: every step after the first is skippable, "not today" is always a button. |
| **Self-publishing is power.** | The drafts folder is home. Publishing is deliberate, reversible and two-step. No like counts, no follower counts, no algorithmic feed. |
| **Humour is a coping tool.** "Sadness from inside out" is a valid mood. | Jokes live on tone dial levels 1–3. They switch off automatically in sensitive contexts. "Chaos goblin" and "running on fumes" are real mood options. |
| **Community beats optimisation.** | Reactions are words ("felt this", "same", "sending snacks"), and counts are visible only to the author. |
| **Honesty over polish.** | Grain, tape, tilt and hand-drawn icons are core tokens. Gloss, gradients, glow and stock smiles are banned. |

## 4. Characters: "the editors"

Slightly alien sprout kids in streetwear: flat colour, 3px ink linework, white die-cut sticker outline, oversized sneakers, baggy denim. They're anime/webcomic-adjacent. **They are guides and companions. They never lecture, never coach, never hold a clipboard, never point at you.**

| Name | Mood | Look | Use for | Never |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sprout** | Hyped, chaotic-good | Spiky green head, yellow hoodie, navy jeans | Onboarding hellos, wins, marketing, "you posted your first draft" | Sad or sensitive moments |
| **Doze** | Tired, unbothered | Hood up, drooping leaf, half-lidded eyes | Night mode, rest prompts, empty states, low-energy days | Urgent errors |
| **Moss** | Soft, shy, sincere | Round mossy head, little yellow flowers, cream hoodie | Journaling, gentle check-ins, **the only character allowed in sensitive states** | Jokes |
| **Static** | Chaotic, glitchy, self-aware | Wild spikes, navy hoodie with yellow stripes, mismatched eyes | Recoverable errors, chaos moods, offline | Anything about safety, health or money |

Each character has six expressions: hyped, okay, soft, tired, chaotic, heavy (`<Character kind mood />`).

## 5. Tone of voice

**Lowercase-friendly. Short sentences. Punchy fragments.** BIG DECLARATIVE HEADLINES with small, soft, honest body copy. Humour first, then sincerity. Always specific, never generic.

- Talk like a friend, not a clinician. No "optimise your wellbeing journey", no toxic positivity.
- Use slang sparingly and never forced. If it sounds like a brand trying to be cool, cut it.
- Never joke at the user's expense during a vulnerable moment.
- Buttons are verbs that say what happens: "post it", "save to drafts", "not today".

### The tone dial

| Level | Name | Where | Rules | Type voices | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **loud** | Marketing, campaigns, posters, social | Big declaratives; one joke per surface; provocative but kind underneath | Display + brush + stickers | Slap, stamp, wiggle |
| 2 | **playful** | Onboarding, success, empty states | Lowercase; humour first, then the useful bit; characters can talk | Display + workhorse, one brush accent | Pop, short slap |
| 3 | **friendly** | Default UI, settings, forms, buttons | Plain verbs; short; slang only if clearer | Workhorse (display for page titles) | Snappy UI only |
| 4 | **calm** | Errors, privacy, account, payments, deletion | What happened, then what to do; never blame; one light touch allowed if recovery is easy | Workhorse only | Fades, no bounce |
| 5 | **gentle** | Distress, crisis, health content, grief, "heavy" check-ins | No jokes, slang or stickers; sentence case; plain words; support visible without scrolling; no timers | Workhorse only | Fades only |

### Safety rule

In sensitive contexts (distress, self-harm, eating, grief, health content), **drop the jokes, use calm, plain language, and surface support clearly.** The dial locks at 5. A "heavy" check-in switches the component into gentle mode automatically. Helpline links must be localised and clinically reviewed before launch.

### Do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't | Why |
|---|---|---|
| you're allowed to cancel plans | Prioritise your needs | Specific beats generic |
| logged. that counts. | Great job! You're crushing your wellness goals! 🎉 | No toxic positivity or optimisation language |
| heavy days are real. | Turn that frown upside down! | Never joke during vulnerable moments |
| how's the weather in there? | Rate your mood from 1–10 | Expressive, not clinical |
| not today | Skip (you'll lose your streak) | No guilt. We don't do streaks |
| we broke something. not you, us. | Error 500: Invalid request | Human, and takes the blame |
| say more? (or don't) | Journal entry (required) | Optional is the default for feelings |
| find your people | Join our wellness community today | If it sounds like an ad, cut it |
| it's out there. you made a thing. | Your post has been successfully submitted. | Celebrate the human, not the system |
| you're offline. your draft is saved on this phone. | Oops! Something went wrong :( | Say what happened and what's safe |
| (plain words) | no cap this app is bussin fr fr | Forced slang = brand trying to be cool |
| You can talk to someone right now. | lol rough day? we got u 💀 | Gentle contexts drop jokes and slang |

## 6. Visual language (summary; detail in `/foundations`)

- **Colour:** **two key colours, Sunshine Yellow and Midnight Navy.** Yellow is the hero; navy does the heavy lifting. Cream, white and ink are quiet neutrals. Red is for errors only. Green lives only in the characters. Yellow on white is banned for text.
- **Type:** three voices. Loud Display (Anton, caps), Hand-drawn Brush (Permanent Marker, 1–6 words) and Quiet Workhorse (Archivo). At most 2 per screen + a brush accent.
- **Shape:** sharp corners for zine and editorial, pillowy for UI. **Strokes sparingly**: shape comes from fill + hard offset sticker shadow (no blur, ever). Strokes only on inputs, ticks, focus and "missing" states.
- **Motion:** slap, stamp, peel, wiggle. UI under 300ms. Reduced motion means fades.
- **Layout:** controlled chaos. Collage, overlap and −3° to 3° tilt, always on a 12-column, 8pt grid.

## 7. Imagery

**Photography.** Candid, selfie-style, flash-lit, slightly imperfect. Real people with personality: statement sunglasses, hoods, drinks, funny faces. Crop tight. Collaged photos get the white die-cut outline.
*Not:* stock smiles, yoga on a cliff, sad person by a window, soft-focus mug hands, anything that looks like a pharmacy ad.

**Meme and screenshot culture.** Tweets, quote cards, reply threads and reaction images are legitimate brand content. Use `<ScreenshotCard>` so the text stays real (accessible, translatable). **Get consent for real people's posts**, or recreate them with fictional handles.

**Illustration.** The editors only. Flat colour, ink line, die-cut. No gradients, no 3D, no "coach" poses.

**Texture.** Photocopy grain, halftone, tape strips, diagonal hazard stripes, torn paper, riso overprint (navy over yellow). One or two per surface, never all at once, and never on forms.

## 8. Principles (tie-breakers)

1. Real over polished.
2. Loud, but never at the user's emotional expense.
3. Chaos lives on a grid.
4. Warmth is non-negotiable.
5. Accessibility is part of the aesthetic, not a compromise.
6. If it looks like a wellness-app template, redo it.
